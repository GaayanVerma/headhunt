## Key Strengths & Gaps

**Key strengths:** Arvind's execution record at a single company is deceptively strong — replacing 80% of vendor workflows, cutting inference costs 97%, and shipping face recognition that outperforms incumbent vendor solutions are outcomes that demonstrate real architectural ownership, not just feature delivery. The interviewer note ("top recommendation", "outstanding culture fit", "driven by scope and impact over compensation") is unusually strong and corroborates what the resume signals. His self-awareness about quality-over-speed tradeoffs is a green flag for a high-ownership environment.

**Main gaps:** Entire career at one company (Piramal Finance) — no evidence of navigating org change, joining a pre-PMF team, or operating without established infrastructure. Ambition score is tempered by the absence of career risk-taking; the desire for a smaller environment is expressed but untested. No GitHub or open portfolio for independent verification of technical depth.
